MFR-Lightning - free early-'80s thrash rhythm rig by Music For Robots
https://musicforrobots.com
Source code: https://github.com/music-for-robots/MFR-Lightning

INSTALLING (zip version)
  Copy the MFR-Lightning.vst3 folder into:
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  Then rescan plugins in your DAW (in FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins
  > Find plugins). MFR-Lightning.exe is a standalone version: pick your
  audio interface under Options, and plug your guitar in.

WHAT IT DOES
  A gate, a mid-hump overdrive, a cranked British master-volume head, a
  4x12 and a plate reverb: tight, scooped, palm-muted thrash rhythm.

  GATE       Keeps the stops tight. All the way down = off.
  OVERDRIVE  ON, Drive, Tone, Level. Leave Drive low and Level high: it
             tightens and pushes the amp rather than adding fizz.
  AMP        Preamp and Master. AMP + CAB off = just the gate and
             overdrive, for your own amp sim.
  EQ         Bass, Middle, Treble, Presence (0-10). The defaults are
             the scooped thrash setting.
  PLATE      Reverb amount (0 = dry).
  OUT        Output level. INIT resets every control.

  Knobs: double-click or Alt-click resets to the default.
         Ctrl- or Shift-drag gives fine control.
         Click the value under a knob to type in a number.
